Chief Minister Handicrafts Pension Scheme 
 
About: 
The scheme was started with the objective of qualitatively improving the standard of living 
and economic condition of the handicraftsmen of the state and encouraging traditional 
arts. Under the scheme, selected craftsmen are given a pension of Rs. 500 / - per month. 
The minimum age of eligible artisans should be 60 years or more. Age relaxation of 05 
years is permissible for women handicraftsmen and physically handicapped craftsmen. 
 
Benefits: 
▪ pension of Rs. 500 / - per month. 
Eligibility: 
▪ The minimum age of eligible artisans should be 60 years or more 
Apply Link: Applicants can apply directly from DI UP portal through given Link. 
https://www.diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in/ 
Documents Required 
• Identity proof i.e. Aadhaar Card, Voter ID Card, etc. 
• Proof of age 
• Caste certificate, if applicable 
• Disability certificate, if applicable 
• Any other document, if required.
