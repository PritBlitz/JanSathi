Chief Minister Mass Marriage Scheme
1. History of the scheme
The Chief Minister Mass Marriage Scheme (Mukhymantri Samuhik Vivah Yojana) has been operational since the financial year 2017-18.
2. Objective of the scheme
The Uttar Pradesh government launched the Mukhymantri Samuhik Vivah Yojana to assist in the marriage of daughters from economically weaker families in the state and in the remarriage of widows, abandoned women, and divorced women. Under this scheme, the state government spends a total of ₹1,00,000 on the marriage of each beneficiary couple.
3. Scheme Benefits
Under this scheme, the Uttar Pradesh government provides financial assistance of ₹1,00,000 per couple.
Out of which ₹60,000 is transferred to the bride's bank account.
Wedding gifts worth ₹25,000 are provided to the bride and groom at the time of marriage.
₹15,000 per couple is spent on event expenses such as food, pandal, furniture, drinking water, electricity/lighting, and other necessary arrangements.
4.Eligibility for the scheme
Age - The applicant girl/woman must be 18 years of age or older, and the groom must be 21 years of age or older.
Category - SC/ST/OBC/General/Minority All categories
Income – The family's annual income must be up to ₹3 lakh.
Domicile – The applicant must be a native of Uttar Pradesh.
Special Criteria-
The benefits of this scheme will be extended only to girls whose marriage has been arranged and who have not been previously married.
Women who have been legally divorced are also eligible to avail the benefits of this scheme for remarriage.
Widowed women are also eligible to avail the benefits of this scheme for remarriage.
5.Required Documents
Aadhaar Card (Bride and Groom)/Aadhaar Linked Mobile Number/Aadhaar Card should not contain an old or childhood photo.
Income Certificate of the Bride's Family
Address proof
Caste Certificate (in case of SC/ST/OBC)
Photograph of the Bride and Groom
Mobile Number
Bank Account Details
6. Application Process
Process to Apply Online Under the Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister's Mass Marriage Scheme:
Step 1: First, visit the official website of the Chief Minister's Mass Marriage Scheme at https://cmsvy.upsdc.gov.in
Step 2: The website's homepage will open. Click on the link under "Apply" on the homepage and perform Aadhaar authentication by entering the bride and groom's Aadhaar card number and date of birth for e-KYC, entering the OTP received on the registered mobile number, and entering the captcha code.
Step-3: Now a form will open in front of you, in this form you have to fill the applicant's personal details, marriage details, annual income details and bank details and upload the required documents, after this enter the captcha code given on the screen and click on the "SUBMIT" button.
