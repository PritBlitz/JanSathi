Marriage Grant Scheme
Grant Scheme for Marriage of Daughters of Scheduled Caste and General Category Poor Persons
1. History of the scheme
The grant scheme for marriage of daughters of poor people of Scheduled Caste and General category is being operated from the financial year 2016-17.
2. Objective of the scheme
The Uttar Pradesh Government has started the Marriage Grant Scheme for the daughters of poor persons of Scheduled Caste and General Category to provide financial assistance for the marriage of daughters of economically weaker Scheduled Caste and General Category poor families of the state.
3. Benefits of the scheme
The amount of financial assistance payable under this scheme per marriage ₹20,000 (Rupees twenty thousand only) is provided. Financial assistance is provided for a maximum of two weddings in a family.
4. Eligibility for the scheme
The income of the applicant should be within the poverty line under the relevant scheme run by the Social Welfare Department, i.e. in the urban area ₹56,460/- per annum and in rural areas should not exceed Rs. 46080/- per annum.
Applicants receiving old age pension, destitute widow pension, disability pension will not require income certificate.
It will be mandatory for the applicants of Scheduled Caste category to enter the number of the caste certificate issued online by the Tehsil.
In the application made for marriage, it is mandatory that the age of the daughter should be 18 years or more on the date of marriage and the age of the groom should be 21 years or more.
Preference will be given to destitute women or disabled applicants after the death of their husbands. Grant will be admissible for the marriage of a maximum of two daughters from a family.
The application will be accepted only 90 days before or 90 days after the date of marriage but the said period will be calculated between the financial year i.e. 01 April to 31 March.
Any of applications filed in the financial year from any from the applicant in that financial year due to technical reasons or budget Financial assistance is not available due to lack of So Financial assistance will be provided to that applicant in the next financial year.money Will not be payable.
5. Required Documents
Before filling out the online application, applicants must have their Aadhaar card, mobile number linked to it, and documents such as caste certificate, income certificate, bank passbook, and marriage card. Caste certificates are not required for general category applicants.
6. Application Process
Applicants (parents/guardians) must register on the Shadi Anudan portal at https://shadianudan.upsdc.gov.in by entering their Aadhaar number, submitting the application online through Aadhaar authentication, and submitting the signed hard copy of the application to the office of the concerned Sub-Divisional Magistrate (urban area)/Block Development Officer (rural area) within 7 days.
