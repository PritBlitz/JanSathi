Highlights of UP Budget 2026-27 
 
The total size of the Budget for the financial year 2026-2027 is ₹9,12,696.35 
crore (₹9 lakh 12 thousand 696 crore 35 lakh), which is ₹1,03,960.29 crore (₹1 
lakh 3 thousand 960 crore 29 lakh), 12.9 percent, higher than the Budget 
Estimates of ₹8,08,736.06 crore for the financial year 2025-2026. 
 
The Budget provides estimated allocations of ₹6,64,470.55 crore for revenue 
expenditure and ₹2,48,225.80 crore for capital expenditure. 
 
Out of the total capital expenditure, an allocation of ₹1,77,744.12 crore is being 
proposed for capital outlay. It is noteworthy that capital outlay leads to the 
creation of assets and employment opportunities. 
 
The total estimated receipts in the Budget amount to ₹8,48,233.18 crore, which 
include revenue receipts of ₹7,28,928.12 crore and capital account receipts of 
₹1,19,305.06 crore. 
 
The Budget also includes new schemes worth ₹43,565.33 crore.

Provision of Budget for Various Departments in the Financial Year 2026–2027 
Farmers 
● 
The State Government has made a record sugarcane price payment of over ₹3,04,321 
crore. This payment is ₹90,802 crore more than the combined sugarcane price 
payment of ₹2,13,519 crore in the previous 22 years. 
● 
For the crushing season 2025-2026, sugarcane price rates have been increased by 
₹30 per quintal. Due to this, sugarcane farmers will receive an additional payment of 
approximately ₹3,000 crore. 
● 
In the Rabi Marketing Year 2025-2026, ₹2,512 crore was paid while purchasing 10.27 
lakh metric tonnes of wheat from farmers. 
● 
In the Kharif Marketing Year 2025-2026, ₹9,710 crore was paid to the farmers while 
purchasing 42.96 lakh metric tonnes of paddy.  
● 
In the Kharif Marketing Year 2025-2026, 2.14 lakh metric tonnes of millet were 
purchased from 54,253 farmers and payments of ₹595 crore were made to the 
farmers. 
● 
From 1 April 2023, farmers are being provided free electricity for irrigation through tube 
wells. 
● 
In the year 2025-2026, under the short-term crop loan distribution scheme, loans worth 
₹10,257 crore were disbursed up to 19 December 2025, benefiting 15.01 lakh farmers. 
● 
In the year 2025-2026, against a target of ₹600 crore for long-term loan distribution, 
loans worth ₹205 crore were disbursed up to 30 November 2025, benefiting 6,870 
farmers. 
● 
Under the 'Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana' from 2017-2018 to 2024-2025, 
approximately 62 lakh farmers received compensation payments totaling ₹5,110 crore. 
In 2025-2026, under the Kharif season, compensation amounting to ₹215 crore has 
been paid to 2.69 lakh insured farmers up to December 2025. 
● 
Under the 'Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana' in the financial year 2025-
2026, up to December 2025, approximately ₹94,668 crore was transferred directly to 
the bank accounts of 3.12 crore farmers through DBT. 
Women

approximately 60,000 women sugarcane farmers across the state.

●

across 29.51 Lakh hectares. 
● 
The 122 sugar mills operating in the state crushed 956 Lakh 9 thousand tons of 
sugarcane, producing 92 lakh 45 thousand tons of sugar. 
●

Pipraich, Munderwan, and Ramala, and more than 44 sugar mills have been 
modernised, creating a total of 125,000 tcd of additional crushing capacity. This 
modernisation and industrial revival of the mills has generated more than 10 Lakh jobs.
