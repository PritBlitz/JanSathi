History of the Scheme
Social Welfare Department The Chief Minister's Abhyudaya Yojana was announced by the Uttar Pradesh government in January 2021 and has been formally implemented since February 16, 2021. Through competitive exams, the program seeks to give underprivileged and economically disadvantaged students in the state access to the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Police Service (IPS), and other elite civil services. Initially, online classes were prioritized, but offline classes were subsequently expanded. Currently, the scheme is active throughout the state.
Objective of the scheme
To achieve the objectives of a welfare state, the scheme aims to enhance the skills and overall upliftment of talented and enthusiastic youth from all sections of the state by providing them with high-level face-to-face (offline) and online guidance and training for various competitive examinations. The scheme aims to provide free coaching, guidance, and resources to meritorious students from economically weaker sections to prepare for UPSC, UPPSC, NDA/CDS, JEE (Mains), NEET, and other competitive examinations. This scheme aims to provide self-reliance to students by addressing financial barriers and encouraging social upliftment and empowerment. Youth from rural and underprivileged areas are given special priority. 500 IAS, 450 IPS, and 300 IFS officers provide assistance in-person (offline) and online.
Benefits of the scheme
Free coaching: Training is provided in both online (through e-learning platforms and virtual classrooms) and offline modes.
Expert Guidance: Personalized mentoring by IAS, IPS, IFS, PCS officers, information on exam patterns and interview preparation.
Digital Resources: Online study material, live sessions, webinars, panel discussions, doubt clearance sessions and career counselling.
other facilities: Career counselling sessions in every district, classes by subject experts these benefits provide students with opportunities for higher education and government jobs.

Implementation of the plan (phased)
Step 1: Structural Framework
Role of the Implementation Partner: Chhatrapati Shahuji Maharaj Research and Training Institute, Lucknow, will be given the responsibility of technical and educational implementation of the scheme.
Step 2: Development of infrastructure and technical setup
Creation of e-learning portal and mobile app: A high-end e-learning portal and mobile application will be developed on which all study materials will be made available free of cost.
Creation of high-quality e-content: Animated videos, audio-visual presentations, notes, test series, and practice materials will be prepared as per the syllabus of competitive examinations.
Establishment of Study Centers: Faculty centers will be established in each district. These centers will be equipped with a library and at least three classrooms for classes. Buildings at state-run or aided colleges can be used for this purpose.
Step 3: Conducting the Academic Process
Hybrid Learning Model: The scheme will be conducted in both online (e-learning portal) and offline (face-to-face classes at faculty centers) modes.
Expert Guidance: Guidance will be provided at the faculty centers by serving and retired IAS and PCS officers and subject experts.
Experienced teachers will be invited as guest lecturers on an honorarium per lecture.
Career Counselling and Guidance: Counselling will also be provided to the students for career selection, orientation development, and skill development.
Step 4: Eligibility, Selection and Registration
Eligibility Criteria: Eligibility will be different for different exams (e.g., Class 11-12 students for JEE/NEET, graduate students for civil services, etc.).
Selection Process: Students for most courses will be selected through an entrance exam, merit, or direct admission. Candidates who pass the Civil Services/PCS Main Examination will be eligible for direct admission.
