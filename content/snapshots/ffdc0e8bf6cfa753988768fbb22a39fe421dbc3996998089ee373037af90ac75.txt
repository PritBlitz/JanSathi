Ministry of Rural Development
Automatic exclusion criteria for Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Gramin Scheme beneficiaries
Posted On:
03 DEC 2024 3:52PM by PIB Delhi
The Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana- Gramin (PMAY-G), is a flagship scheme of the Government of India and it is being implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development since 1 st April 2016. The Cabinet approved the construction of 2.95 crore houses under PMAY-G by March 2024 with basic amenities along with the use of SECC 2011 data for identification of beneficiaries. A committee was constituted to finalize the Guidelines i.e. Framework for implementation (FFI) of the PMAY-G comprising members from State/UT Governments.
The Union Cabinet in its meeting held on 09.08.2024 approved 2 crore additional houses to be constructed during FY 2024-25 to FY 2028-29 under PMAY-G. The Union Cabinet had also approved the modified exclusion criteria for identification of additional eligible households. As per the Union Cabinet approval, the provisions with regard to mechanised two wheelers, mechanised fishing boat, landline phone and refrigerator have been deleted. Further, the income criteria has also been enhanced from Rs 10000 per month to Rs 15000 and the land related criteria have been simplified.
The details of revised automatic exclusion criteria under the new phase of the PMAY-G are as under:
Step 1: Exclusion of pucca houses- All households living in houses with pucca roof and/or pucca wall and households living in houses with more than 2 rooms are filtered out.
Step 2: Automatic Exclusion criteria– From the remaining set of households, all households fulfilling any one of the 10 parameters listed below are automatically excluded: -
Motorised three/four-wheeler
Mechanised three/ four-wheeler agricultural equipment
Kisan Credit Card with credit limit of Rs. 50,000 or above
Household with any member as a Government employee
Households with non-agricultural enterprises registered with the Government
Any member of the family earning more than Rs.15,000 per month
Paying income tax
Paying professional tax
Own 2.5 acres or more of irrigated land
Own 5 acres or more of unirrigated land.
Under the Scheme, as on date, out of a cumulative target of PMAY-G, 3.33 crore houses have been allocated to the States/UTs and out of which 3.21 crore houses have been sanctioned and more than 2.67 crore houses have been completed. The State-wise details of funds allocated and utilized under PMAY-G is given in Annexure -I and State-wise number of beneficiaries in last five years is given in Annexure-II.
This information was given by the Minister of State for Rural Development, Dr. Chandra Sekhar Pemmasani in a written reply in Lok Sabha today.

<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">The Union Cabinet in its meeting held on 09.08.2024 approved 2 crore additional houses to be constructed during FY 2024-25 to FY 2028-29 under PMAY-G. The Union Cabinet had also approved the modified exclusion criteria for identification of additional eligible households. As per the Union Cabinet approval, the provisions with regard to mechanised two wheelers, mechanised fishing boat, landline phone and refrigerator have been deleted. Further, the income criteria has also been enhanced from Rs 10000 per month to Rs 15000 and the land related criteria have been simplified. </span></span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">The details of revised automatic exclusion criteria under the new phase of the PMAY-G are as under:&nbsp; </span></span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px"><strong>Step 1: Exclusion of pucca houses- </strong>All households living in houses with pucca roof and/or pucca wall and households living in houses with more than 2 rooms are filtered out. </span></span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px"><strong>Step 2: Automatic Exclusion criteria&ndash; </strong>From the remaining set of households, all households fulfilling any one of the 10 parameters listed below are automatically excluded: -</span></span></p>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Motorised three/four-wheeler</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Mechanised three/ four-wheeler agricultural equipment</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Kisan Credit Card with credit limit of Rs. 50,000 or above</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Household with any member as a Government employee</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Households with non-agricultural enterprises registered with the Government</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Any member of the family earning more than Rs.15,000 per month</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Paying income tax</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Paying professional tax</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Own 2.5 acres or more of irrigated land</span></span></li>
<li style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Own 5 acres or more of unirrigated land. </span></span></li>
<p style="text-align:justify">&nbsp;</p>
<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:Times New Roman,Times,serif"><span style="font-size:16px">Under the Scheme, as on date, out of a cumulative target of PMAY-G, 3.33 crore houses have been allocated to the States/UTs and out of which 3.21 crore houses have been sanctioned and more than 2.67 crore houses have been completed. The State-wise details of funds allocated and utilized under PMAY-G is given in <strong>Annexure -I</strong> and State-wise number of beneficiaries in last five years is given in <strong>Annexure-II.</strong></span></span></p>
<p style="text-align:justify"><span style="font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;,Times,serif; font-size:16px">This information was given by the Minister of State for Rural Development, Dr. Chandra Sekhar Pemmasani in a written reply in Lok Sabha today.</span></p>
